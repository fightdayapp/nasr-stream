# Fight Day Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 21, 2026  
**Publisher:** Nasr Corp  
**App:** Fight Day  
**Android package:** `com.fightdayapp`

Nasr Corp (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the Fight Day mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains what information Fight Day uses, where it is stored, when third-party services may receive information, and the choices available to users.

## 1. Information stored locally on your device

Fight Day is designed so that most training information stays on your device.

The app may store locally:

- training goal, level, focus, and equipment selections;
- equipment weights and workout customizations;
- workout completion and training-history information;
- sparring and cardio timer settings and history;
- reminders and notification preferences;
- language and appearance preferences;
- locally cached premium-entitlement status;
- app configuration and backup/import settings.

This information is stored using Android local app storage. Nasr Corp does not operate a user-account system for Fight Day and does not automatically upload your workout history to Nasr Corp servers.

You can remove locally stored Fight Day data by using the app’s reset tools where available, clearing the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

## 2. Backup and restore

Fight Day may allow you to export a backup file containing app settings and training history. The exported file is created only when you choose to export it and is saved to a location you select through Android.

Fight Day does not automatically upload exported backup files to Nasr Corp. If you choose to store or share a backup through another service, that service’s privacy terms apply.

## 3. Advertising

The free version of Fight Day may display advertising using Google AdMob / the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information such as:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
- app and ad interactions;
- diagnostic and performance information;
- device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising identifier where available.

This information may be used by Google and its advertising partners for advertising, analytics, measurement, and fraud prevention, subject to Google’s policies and your consent choices.

Fight Day does not sell your workout history or training preferences to advertisers.

Where required by law or Google policy, Fight Day will request advertising/privacy consent before serving ads that require consent. You may also be given a privacy-options entry point to review or change applicable consent choices.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Purchases and Fight Day Pro

Fight Day uses Google Play Billing for digital purchases such as Fight Day Pro or Founder/Lifetime access.

Purchases are processed by Google Play. Nasr Corp does not receive your full payment-card or bank-account details.

Fight Day may receive and store information needed to determine your entitlement, such as:

- product purchased;
- purchase state;
- purchase token or transaction-related identifier;
- whether the purchase has been acknowledged;
- locally cached entitlement status.

Fight Day may use Google Play services and Play Integrity to help validate purchases, app authenticity, and protect against fraud or unauthorized access. If server-side verification is enabled, a purchase token and integrity token may be sent to a Fight Day verification service solely for entitlement and security verification.

## 5. Language packs and on-device translation

Fight Day includes English and French directly and may prepare additional language packs using Google ML Kit on-device translation.

ML Kit language models may be downloaded to your device. Translation is performed on the device after the required model is available. Fight Day stores the completed language pack locally so it can be used offline.

Google ML Kit is provided by Google and is subject to Google’s applicable terms and privacy practices.

## 6. Notifications

Fight Day may request permission to send notifications for training reminders and timer-related notifications.

Reminder schedules and reminder text are stored locally on your device. You can disable notifications through Fight Day settings or Android system settings.

## 7. Internet access

Fight Day can perform core workout and timer functions offline. Internet access may be used for:

- loading advertisements;
- downloading a language model or preparing a language pack;
- Google Play purchase and ownership checks;
- Play Integrity or purchase verification;
- other Google Play services required for those features.

## 8. Information Fight Day does not request

Fight Day does not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or SMS messages for its normal workout features.

Fight Day does not require a Nasr Corp account to record workouts.

## 9. Data sharing

Nasr Corp does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers only where needed for app functionality, including:

- Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for advertising;
- Google Play Billing for purchases;
- Google Play Integrity for security and anti-fraud;
- Google ML Kit for downloadable on-device language models.

Those providers process information under their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

## 10. Data retention

Training data and settings stored by Fight Day remain on your device until you delete them, reset the app, clear app storage, or uninstall the app.

Purchase records are also maintained by Google Play according to Google’s policies. Third-party services may retain information according to their own retention policies.

## 11. Security

Fight Day uses Android application storage for local data and uses HTTPS/TLS-capable Google services where network communication is required.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 12. Children’s privacy

Fight Day is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to Nasr Corp by a child in connection with Fight Day, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

## 13. International processing

Third-party providers such as Google may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their privacy policies describe their international data-transfer practices and safeguards.

## 14. Your choices

Depending on the feature and your region, you may be able to:

- change or remove Fight Day training data from within the app;
- clear all app storage through Android settings;
- disable notifications;
- manage advertising/privacy choices when the consent interface is available;
- reset or delete the Android advertising ID through Android privacy settings;
- remove the app by uninstalling it.

## 15. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Fight Day features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.
